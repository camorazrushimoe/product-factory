# Skills — Adversarial Reviewer

## Core skills

### 1. review-problem-statements
Check that Problem Statements are real, specific, and evidence-based (not restated wishes).

### 2. review-goals-and-metrics
Verify that goals have measurable success metrics and realistic targets.

### 3. review-scope
Detect vague, oversized, or poorly bounded scope. Force clear In / Out of Scope.

### 4. find-contradictions
Find logical contradictions between problem, solution, constraints and assumptions.

### 5. challenge-assumptions
Surface unstated assumptions and demand they are made explicit.

### 6. quality-verdict
Produce a structured review with verdict (approve / needs-changes / major-rework) and prioritized findings.
